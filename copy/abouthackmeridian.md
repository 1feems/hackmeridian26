### Background

Hack Meridian is Stellar’s flagship pre-Meridian builder experience, bringing together 450+ developers, founders, and existing Stellar teams to build, submit, and advance new projects.

The opportunity is to create more than a two-day hackathon. Hack Meridian will serve as a structured entry point into the Stellar ecosystem, moving participants from regional recruitment and onboarding through project completion, Meridian participation, and post-event opportunities such as Drips, Instawards, funding, and ecosystem introductions.

## Hack Meridian Lisbon 2026

**Hackathon:** October 25–26, 2026

**Meridian Day 0:** October 27, 2026

**Meridian:** October 28–29, 2026

**Location:** Lisbon, Portugal

## Start Date: August 10, 2026

### Success Criteria

- 500+ qualified builders attend in person
- Venue and infrastructure can support up to 500 hackers
- Strong conversion from registration to attendance
- High project submission and completion rate
- Three Genesis and three Scale finalists selected
- Participants remain engaged through Meridian
- High-potential teams transition into Drips, Instawards, funding, or ecosystem support
- Zero critical Wi-Fi, power, safety, submission, or production failures
- Positive feedback from Stellar, builders, mentors, judges, and partners
- Clear post-event reporting and ownership of ecosystem follow-up
